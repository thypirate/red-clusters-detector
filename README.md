# Red Cluster Analysis

## Context

The images of pigment particles in a plasticol were obtained by using Scanning electron microscopy (SEM) to analyse the distribution withing the material.

A binarization step was first applied, converting the original images to black and white in order to highlight the pigment particles against the background.

From these binarized images, it was possible to identify regions where particles are more concentrated, corresponding to clusters. Comparison across different samples revealed variations in the degree of pigment dispersion, with a greater degree of cluster formation visible in some cases.

The goal of this analysis was to identify these clusters, as they are associated with the occurrence of color defects during the plastisol curing process. This allowed a qualitative relationship to be established between particle distribution and the final quality of the material.
